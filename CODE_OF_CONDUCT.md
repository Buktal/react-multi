# Code of conduct

ReAct-M welcomes participants of all backgrounds and experience levels. These expectations apply to issues, pull requests and other project interactions.

## Participation

- Treat others with respect and assume good intent when asking for clarification.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about code and ideas.
- Respect privacy and obtain permission before sharing another person's information.
- Accept corrections and keep disagreements focused on the technical question.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Use GitHub's **Report content** option for abusive content. See [Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for private reporting to GitHub. General repository moderation concerns can be raised in an issue without disclosing personal information.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, lock conversations, or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of the behavior and give participants a clear explanation where practical.
